GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A few details before you open the Studio.

What does AI MIDI Generator create?+

Editable multitrack MIDI arrangements from natural-language prompts or custom musical settings, including basslines, chords, melodies, arpeggios, pads and other instrument tracks.

Can I control tempo, key and scale?+

Yes. The Studio includes controls for BPM, key, scale, rhythmic complexity and section structure.

Can I edit and export the generated MIDI?+

Yes. Preview and edit tracks, import an existing MIDI file, export MIDI and use the Studio's WAV export tools.

Can I download MIDI demos for free?+

Yes. You can load, preview and download MIDI demos from the MIDI Demo Library without connecting Pollinations or spending Pollen. Pollinations is only needed for AI features such as generating or editing MIDI.

Are generations free?+

The Studio is accessible without a subscription, but it does not include free generation credits. Generations use your own Pollinations Pollen through Pollinations Pay. You approve the budget when connecting your account.

Does this replace my DAW?+

No. It complements your workflow. Export MIDI into Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic Pro or another compatible DAW for sound design, arrangement and mixing.
